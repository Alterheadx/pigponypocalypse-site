About the game

A boss rush that punishes every mistake

PigPonyPocalypse is four hand-built boss fights and nothing else. No levels, no grind, no trash enemies. Just you, the boss's patterns, and a global DPS leaderboard.

Every boss plays by its own rules. Some attacks can only be survived behind cover. Casts have to be interrupted. Curses stack while you get greedy with damage. Floor tiles explode in an order you'll need to memorize.

Forgiving difficulty is there to learn the fight. True Challenge is the real game: new phases, new deadly mechanics and a different rhythm for every boss.

Beating a boss is half the job. Your best DPS on each boss and difficulty goes to a global Steam leaderboard, so the fight keeps going after the kill: pick from nine talents, find a better opener, squeeze out more damage than everyone else.

You don't fight alone, but your party won't make it easier. Facewall the tank dreams of getting punched in the face and rates every boss on its hits. Shawty the healer flat-out refuses to heal him. Command both, and freeze time when everything falls apart at once.

Every death is on you

Every mechanic can be solved. No random one-shots, no stat checks you can grind past.

The hard mode is a different fight: extra phases, new mechanics and tighter timings on every boss.

The bosses, in order

Four fights, each with its own rules

Boss 1 of 4

Arcane Corrupted Piglet

Looks harmless. Corrupts the ground under your hooves, summons towers and boars, and on True Challenge adds a third phase built around Arcane Annihilation.

  • Darkness — the lights go out while Chasing Boars hunt you down.
  • Detonation — someone has to stand in the circle, or everything blows up.
  • Annihilation — the whole arena burns; only the towers give cover.
Boss 2 of 4

Swying Swyan

Twin boars: one is vicious, the other is a little dim. Outrun a wall of fire across a crumbling bridge, then face the brothers in their arena.

  • Wall of Fire — the bridge crumbles and the fire never stops.
  • Flying Boars — they fire homing orbs while you run from the fire.
  • Orb Maze — Swying fills the arena with a maze of deadly fireballs.
Boss 3 of 4

Boar Sentinel

The arena is split in three, and the Sentinel fights differently in each part.

  • Left side — abilities recharge faster, but you take damage every second.
  • Right side — your damage keeps growing, but you take damage every second too.
  • Center — the Sentinel vanishes and gives you a Memory Puzzle to solve.

Enrage: 4 minutes

Boss 4 of 4 · Final

The Swine King

Four phases, one for every quarter of his health. The bosses you defeated and purified come back to fight at your side.

  • Fire Cross — place the spears so you can make it through.
  • Boar General — the King's loyal servant, with his own attacks and kamikaze piglets.
  • Fire Memory — the tiles under your feet explode in an order you'll need to memorize.
  • Royal Pyre — the King turns invulnerable and slowly burns the whole platform. Can you figure out what to do about it?

Enrage: 7.5 minutes
